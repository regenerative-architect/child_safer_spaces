## S8 — Mackey DC et al. (2019) FLIP randomized trial of compliant flooring in long-term care. PLoS Medicine 16(6): e1002843

- Publisher: PLoS Medicine (peer reviewed)
- Link: https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371/journal.pmed.1002843
- Jurisdiction: Canada (single long-term care site)
- Type: Randomized controlled trial
- Edition / date: Published 2019
- Retrieved: 2026-10-20
- Access: Abstract and results text read
- What it supports: 150 rooms randomized; 2.54 cm compliant underlay beneath vinyl versus plywood. Serious fall-related injury rate ratio 1.04 (95% CI 0.45–2.39); falls rate ratio 1.21 (0.87–1.68), i.e. a non-significant point estimate of more falls; adverse outcomes were not assessed.
- Limits: Older adults in long-term care; one product and assembly. Low transferability to children in retail.
- Cited in documents: 04, 27, 32, 50, 51, 52
- Evidence claims: E14
