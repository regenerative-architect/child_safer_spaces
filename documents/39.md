**Workflow:** W09 in document 49. Award needs a signed decision after G2.
## 1. Invitation and objective
[Buyer] invites proposals to address [hazard IDs] at [site/zone] under the Child-Safer Spaces program. Bids may propose repair, redesign, operational improvement or a purpose-designed protective assembly. Selection is based on demonstrated suitability and whole-life cost; no particular foam or brand is required.
## 2. Scope and site information
Provide site access for assessment at [time]. Buyer supplies [plan, exposure, loads, substrate facts, cleaning methods, known constraints]. Bidder verifies conditions relevant to its design and lists assumptions. Scope includes design coordination, applicable submissions, mock-up/testing, installation, in-situ verification, commissioning, training, maintenance instructions, traceability and removal/restoration planning. Exclusions must be explicit.
## 3. Required submission
Technical approach and alternatives; exact system/configuration; compliance and approval assumptions; independent evidence and limits; completed acceptance schedule (document 08); risk/failure analysis; work and closure plan; accessibility, route-geometry, cleaning and load compatibility; qualifications; warranty/remedies; itemized pricing; recurring/replacement/removal costs; lead times; conflicts; references for comparable uses. Supply reports, not marketing summaries.
## 4. Evaluation and clarification
Mandatory criteria in document 08 are pass/fail. Unresolved mandatory evidence prevents award for public installation. Weighted scoring applies only to eligible submissions (document 09). The buyer may request clarification or independent testing; changes are documented and shared fairly with bidders. Do not turn a lowest-price decision into a claim of safety equivalence.
## 5. Deliverables and hold points
D1 survey and design; D2 accepted test/approval plan; D3 mock-up and dossier; D4 installation records; D5 in-situ verification, commissioning and required inspections; D6 training and handover; D7 scheduled follow-up; D8 defects/removal support. No concealed work or public reopening past a hold point without the designated written release.
## 6. Commercial schedule for legal completion
Contract price/currency [ ]; taxes/freight [ ]; milestones and retainage [ ]; insurance [ ]; permits [ ]; defects response [ ]; warranty [ ]; change control [ ]; recall/product-change notices [ ]; ownership of reports [ ]; confidentiality/privacy [ ]; indemnity/liability [ ]; termination/restoration [ ]; dispute/governing law [ ]. Local counsel completes and aligns this with the actual contract.
## 7. Acceptance
Acceptance requires evidence against every applicable criterion, resolved critical defects and signed operator and technical releases. Payment, use of the space or a vendor certificate is never a substitute for the agreed acceptance process. Preserve statutory rights and duties as determined by counsel.
