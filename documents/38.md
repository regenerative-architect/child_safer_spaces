## Proposed structure
Create a steering group chaired by the operator/sponsor, with facilities, frontline workers, accessibility and disability representatives, community caregivers, insurer/risk advisors and independent evaluation expertise. Invite authorities within their remit without implying endorsement. Suppliers contribute technical information but do not adjudicate their own evidence.
## Decisions and accountability
The operator keeps responsibility for safe premises and work. The steering group coordinates, budgets and reviews the program; it cannot waive law or shift duties to unpaid advisors. Professional reviewers sign only within their competence. Authorities keep their statutory powers. Public participation is advisory unless a specific governance agreement says otherwise. Decision rights are set out in document 49.

## Independence controls
Declare financial and professional interests at appointment and whenever an agenda item changes them. Recuse conflicted members from supplier award and outcome judgement. Contracts should preserve access to necessary data and the right to report adverse or neutral findings within agreed privacy and confidentiality limits. Never allow a funder to rewrite scientific conclusions.
## Assurance plan
First line: staff inspections and manager follow-up. Second line: corporate risk/facilities review of records and configuration. Third line: independent technical and evaluation audits at defined milestones. These are proposed layers, scaled to program size. Independence must be documented, not inferred from a job title.
## Complaints and escalation
Offer accessible channels, acknowledge complaints within a locally adopted target, triage urgent hazards immediately, and report resolution without exposing personal details. Record complaints about accessibility, cleaning, worker burden and communications as safety information. Allow dissent in decision records. Escalate unresolved critical conditions to the sponsor and the relevant authority as required.
## Program identity
Use "participating pilot site" only while participation is real and current. Do not issue "certified child-safe" badges. A future certification scheme would need separate standards, independent conformity assessment, surveillance, appeals, misuse controls and governance; none is created by this kit.
