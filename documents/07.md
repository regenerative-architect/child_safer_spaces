## Draft request — adapt before sending
Subject: Pre-installation review request — Child-Safer Spaces pilot at [site]

We are assessing [specific hazards] at [address, occupancy, tenant/owner]. The proposed work comprises [surface system or fixture change, edge protection, extent, substrate, attachment, transitions]. We request guidance on the applicable adopted requirements, permit pathway and inspections before purchase or installation.

Attached: marked plan with accessible and egress routes; existing and proposed dimensions, including route widths and protrusions; product and assembly dossier; fire and surface test reports for the configuration proposed; installation and adhesive details; sanitation and maintenance procedure; landlord consent status; commissioning plan and withdrawal design.

Please identify: applicable code edition, amendments and sections; whether the work is a regulated alteration; required drawings or professional seals; the fire-performance test and classification for this assembly and location, separately for floor and fixture elements; accessibility review; health review for food-related zones; permits and inspection hold points; and any conditions or additional evidence needed. Please tell us if any question is outside your authority and which office to consult.

Contact: [authorized project contact]. Intended dates: [ ]. We will not treat silence or receipt as approval, and we can revise or withdraw the proposed system if required.

## Separate decision log
Record submitted, more information requested, conditional approval, approved, rejected or not required, with the written basis, in the consultation register. Do not combine building, fire, accessibility and health permissions into one unsupported "compliant" field. Approval for one site or configuration does not transfer automatically to another.
