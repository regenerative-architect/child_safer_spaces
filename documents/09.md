**Workflow:** W09 in document 49. Requests can be prepared now; award needs G2 and a signed sponsor decision.
## Request for information
Provide product name/version, intended commercial application, exclusions, full assembly, thickness, seams, substrate conditions, installation instructions, cure time, cleaning requirements, service-life assumptions, disposal route and warranty. Supply independent reports with laboratory scope/accreditation where relevant, sample identity, dates, methods and editions, conditions, failures and limitations. Identify every difference between the tested and the offered system, including the added thickness of any guard or padding at real fixtures.
Quote quantities and unit basis, preparation, moisture testing, labour, closures, taxes, freight, training, spare stock, inspections, periodic tests, repairs and removal. State currency, validity, lead time, warranty remedy and who bears failed-acceptance costs. Disclose conflicts and who funded each study. Do not describe a product as "childproof" or rely on playground certification [S7].
## Comparison process
First pass/fail the mandatory legal, fire, accessibility, hygiene, stability and operational criteria (document 08). A cheaper bid cannot offset a failure. For surviving bids use proposed weights: operating suitability 30%, durability/maintenance 25%, whole-life cost 25%, relevant independent evidence 15%, support/traceability 5%. Score 0–5 with written reasons; record dissent and missing evidence. Fix the weights before opening bids.
