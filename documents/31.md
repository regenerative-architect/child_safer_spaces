## One assembly, several reviewers
The same change can affect flame behaviour, escape, cleaning, moisture and access. Facilities coordinates; fire/code, hygiene, accessibility and operations reviewers each decide within their remit. A supplier's "commercial grade" description is not an approval.
## Fire and egress inquiry
Identify occupancy, installation location, floor versus wall/fixture use, thickness, exposed area, substrate, adhesive and proximity to escape routes or heat sources. Ask the code professional which test, classification and edition apply to each element. E648-25a is a floor-covering critical-radiant-flux method developed for corridors and exitways and does not by itself assess fire hazard [S11]; padding on fixtures or walls may fall under different provisions (interior finish, trim, decorative materials, furnishings or foam plastics, depending on the adopted code). Never reuse one material's floor report as evidence for a fixture guard or an untested adhesive combination. Carpet and rug products have their own federal flammability tests in the U.S. [S12], which do not classify every mat or foam product.
Record report scope, specimen match, laboratory, code reference, required class, result and the authority's determination. Confirm that trims, barriers and installation staging keep the escape route. Fire approval does not imply traction or hygiene acceptance.
## Cleaning validation
Before procurement, cleaning staff demonstrate the routine on a mock-up using the actual product, soil, dilution, machine/pad and drying method. Look for residue, tackiness, odour, staining, tearing, slippery film, liquid ingress, inaccessible seams and inability to dry within the operating window. Health or food-safety reviewers set any microbial or other verification; appearance alone cannot demonstrate sanitation.
## Food zones
The FDA Food Code is a model code; the 2026 edition was released on 17 September 2026 [S4][S44]. Determine the locally adopted text and the requirements for the specific food preparation, storage or customer area. Do not assume an allowance or prohibition from another zone or jurisdiction. Obtain the applicable public-health determination for Canadian food premises.
## Worker effort
Softer floors can increase the force needed to move carts, beds and wheelchairs [S35]. Include stocking and cleaning staff in the mock-up and record effort, not just appearance.
## Material and environmental dossier
Request composition restrictions relevant to the market, emissions evidence if applicable, SDS for installation and cleaning chemicals where required, safe curing/ventilation conditions, expected degradation, repair method and disposal instructions. "Non-toxic", "eco-friendly" and "antimicrobial" need defined evidence and do not establish suitability. Do not add antimicrobial claims as a program benefit without relevant support.
## Disposal and continuity
Budget for adhesive removal, substrate restoration, waste segregation and locally applicable disposal. Do not promise recycling without an accepting facility. Keep spare stock and an approved temporary control; never improvise an untested material during a supply shortage.
