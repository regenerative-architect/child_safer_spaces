## Laboratory brief
Test representative as-installed assemblies, including substrate, adhesive, joints and trims where relevant. Document sample provenance, conditioning, aging, contamination, equipment calibration/validation, measurement uncertainty, deviations and raw results. Distinguish accredited methods from bespoke engineering tests and state limitations. Agree conditions and pass criteria with the relevant specialists before testing.
## Standards routing (editions checked 2026-10-20)
F2508-23 validates walkway tribometry and is not intended to establish a "safe" threshold for any surface [S10]. E648-25a measures critical radiant flux of floor-covering systems and was developed for corridors and exitways [S11]; the required method, class and edition depend on the adopted code and location. F1292-26 is a playground surfacing specification focused on head-injury risk [S7]; use it only for a justified play-area application, never as retail certification. Obtain licensed current or adopted texts as needed; public scope pages do not replace test procedures.
## Installer hold points
Verify substrate and environment before work; submit deviations before proceeding; inspect concealed components; keep batch and installation records; protect public routes; meet cure and commissioning requirements. Provide removal instructions that restore a compliant surface.
## Supplier change and recall control
Notify the buyer of changes to composition, thickness, adhesive, source factory or installation instructions that could affect performance. List installed sites and batches in the asset register. Define notice recipients, defect response times, spare-stock availability and recall/removal costs in the contract.
