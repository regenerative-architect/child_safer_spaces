## Customer notice — replace fields before use
We are improving everyday walking areas at [site]. Our work focuses on finding hazards, keeping routes clear, responding to spills, keeping carts in good repair and checking selected design improvements. Some measures are being evaluated in a limited pilot. Please tell a staff member about a loose edge, wet floor, broken cart strap or difficulty moving through an area. For urgent help, use [site assistance route]. Feedback: [accessible channel].
This program does not make an area injury-proof. We will review maintenance, accessibility, customer feedback and incidents before deciding whether to expand it.
## FAQ
Why not cover all floors in foam? Softer materials can move, create seams and edges, be hard to clean, and make carts, strollers and wheelchairs harder to push. We choose controls for the actual hazard and test the installed system.
Will injuries fall by a certain percentage? No local reduction has been shown. We will report findings and limitations.
Is this a certification? No. Participation describes a safety-improvement process, not an independent certification or government endorsement.
How can disabled visitors contribute? Contact [channel] for accessible feedback or a voluntary, compensated advisory session. Safe access remains available during any work, and we post notice of temporary disruptions.

## Claims approval checklist
Verify each factual statement against a dated record in the publication-claims register. Get permission before naming partners or showing logos. Never use "childproof", "zero risk", "certified safe", unsupported percentages or implied insurer/government endorsement. Communications and evaluation leads approve; legal reviews material claims. Offer translated and accessible formats appropriate to the site.
