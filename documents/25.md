**Workflow:** W14 in document 49. Needs a signed sponsor decision.
## Decision options and the evidence each needs
| Option | Use when | Minimum evidence before deciding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STOP / REMOVE | Critical risks remain, the system is unsuitable, or safe maintenance cannot be funded | Unresolved critical finding or failed mandatory criterion; funded removal plan |
| REVISE | An addressable failure needs redesign and retesting | Root cause; corrected design; retest plan against pre-agreed criteria |
| EXTEND EVALUATION | Operations are acceptable but injury evidence is insufficient | Feasibility criteria met; analysis plan showing the exposure an effectiveness claim needs (document 35) |
| SCALE IN STAGES | Evidence, local approvals, capacity and funding all support it | Independent review; no unresolved critical defects; site-by-site approvals; maintenance and removal funded |
Operational feasibility supports further testing, not an injury-reduction claim.

## Gate G6 questions
- Were the intended controls actually used and maintained?
- Are all critical defects and required approvals resolved?
- Are gains distinguishable from reporting, exposure or seasonal changes?
- Does the study support effectiveness, or only feasibility?
- Do child-specific data support any child-specific claim?
- Can new sites replicate the tested configuration and maintenance?
- Are removal, replacement, staff time and independent oversight funded?
- What uncertainty or adverse evidence argues against expansion?
## Controlled expansion plan
Define a new cohort, site eligibility and budget cap; repeat jurisdiction and commissioning reviews for every site. Keep a configuration register and change notices. Monitor weekly at first and at locally approved intervals thereafter. Reopen the decision after adverse trends, material substitution, new evidence or changed use.
