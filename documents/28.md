## Prepare the assessment
Owner: site lead with facilities and worker participation. Gather a floor plan, trading and cleaning schedules, de-identified incident mechanisms, maintenance history, cart fleet condition, known access concerns and planned renovations. Invite disability advisors through an accessible, voluntary and compensated process. Never stage hazards or ask children to demonstrate falls.

## Walk each zone at different operating states
Observe opening, peak traffic, cleaning, restocking and wet weather where safely feasible. Record whether each condition is observed, reported or inferred. Inspect fixture edges, corners and projections from the floor to at least 120 cm for planning ages two to five — about the 97th-percentile height of five-year-olds in the WHO growth standards [S37] — and higher if older children are in scope. Record route widths, protrusions and transition heights for specialist review; a guessed dimension is not a compliance conclusion.
| Zone | Observe | Required record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exterior/entrance | Rain paths, drainage, mat coverage, threshold and drying | Weather, location, water source, maintenance owner |
| Cart corral and seats | Straps present and working, seat condition, wheels, signage | Cart IDs with defects; removal from service |
| Aisles/endcaps | Display bases, stacked stock, projections, visibility | Plan reference, obstruction source, restocking time |
| Queues/service | Pinch points, turns, fixtures, manoeuvring | Busy-period configuration and alternative route |
| Food-adjacent | Grease, spills, cleaning residues, ingress below finish | Contaminant, method, affected food zone |
| Equipment routes | Loaded wheels, turning, braking, stationary loads | Equipment mass/loading and wheel data |
| Separate play area | Boundaries and specialist requirements | Separate design owner; no retail-floor substitution |
## Hazard register completion
Write an observable condition — "entrance mat edge lifting at northwest corner after cart traffic" — rather than "unsafe floor". Record immediate containment, responsible role, permanent remedy, due date, verification and recurrence check. Critical conditions are isolated immediately; the rest of the assessment continues elsewhere.
## Exposure protocol
Count entries over the same times and zones as events. Record sensor placement, exclusion of staff and re-entry, outages and manual validation samples. If only whole-store counts exist, report whole-store rates and do not label them zone rates. Child denominators need a separately approved, credible method. No facial recognition or identity tracking is required or allowed for this program.
## Do not record here
Children's names, ages tied to an event, injury descriptions, photographs showing people, CCTV, witness details or claim numbers. Record only a coded reference to the protected record.
## Review output
Rank recurring mechanisms, flag incomplete records, select one bounded intervention area and a comparable area or site, and issue a signed assessment summary. Keep photographs without people and a dated plan as evidence references in controlled storage.
