## How to use these cards
Each workflow names its trigger, prerequisites, owner, decision authority, inputs, steps, record, acceptance criteria and escalation route. The label says whether the work needs no new approval, needs local review, or needs a signed commitment; gates still run in order. The Workflows view in the workspace shows the same cards with links to documents and registers. This document is generated from source/content.json; edit the workflows there, or keep a local draft here.

## W03 · Initial hazard walk, immediate controls and routing
**No approval needed** · Gate: G0 · Owner: Site manager · Decision authority: Site manager (isolation and closure)
| Part | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | Day 1; any layout, product, season or traffic change; a reported concern. |
| Prerequisites | Existing safety and emergency procedures; barriers and supplies available. |
| Inputs | Floor plan, the 0–120 cm child survey band (document 28), cart-fleet status, hazard checklist. |
| Output or record | Hazard and action rows; routing decisions for adjacent mechanisms. |
| Acceptance or completion | Every critical condition is isolated with an owner and verification; no hazard is kept for research; every adjacent mechanism has a named owner. |
| Exceptions and escalation | No capacity to repair: tell the sponsor the same day. Regulated systems (egress, fire protection, structure) affected: landlord and the authority. |
| Documents | 04, 28, 27 |
| Registers | hazards, actions |
**Steps**
1. Walk every zone at opening, peak, cleaning and restocking where safely feasible, with facilities and a worker representative.
2. Record each observable condition in the hazard register — never people's details.
3. Isolate critical conditions at once with a physical barrier and an accessible alternative; offer assistance.
4. Take defective carts out of service and log them.
5. Assign owners and due dates in the action register.
6. Route carts, tip-over, falling merchandise, doors and escalators to their accountable owners (T35).
## W04 · Daily inspection, spill response and reopening
**No approval needed** · Gate: any · Owner: Shift lead · Decision authority: Shift lead isolates; the designated role reopens after verification
| Part | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Trigger | Scheduled inspection, a spill or contamination report, cleaning, restocking or weather. |
| Prerequisites | Trained responder and deputy on shift; approved cleaning methods; barriers. |
| Inputs | Inspection schedule, approved chemicals and dilution, spill kit. |
| Output or record | Spill-response and inspection rows; handoff rows. |
| Acceptance or completion | All four timestamps recorded; reopening verified by the authorized role; recurring sources escalated. |
| Exceptions and escalation | Recurring source or overdue repair: site manager the same shift. Product defect: suspend under W11. |
| Documents | 12, 13, 47 |
| Registers | spill_response, inspection, handoffs |
**Steps**
1. Protect people: physically block entry and keep an accessible alternative.
2. Offer assistance (for example, retrieve goods from a closed aisle).
3. Identify the hazard; call a specialist for unknown chemicals or blood.
4. Clean and fix the source with the approved method; keep the area closed while wet.
5. Verify dryness, residue, seams and guards before reopening.
6. Log the four timestamps; hand over open items at shift change (W16).
