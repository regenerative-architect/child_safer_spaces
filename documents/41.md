## Illustration only
Example site EX-001 is a fictional family-oriented general retailer. No real company, product, incident history, price, person or approval is represented. Each register in the workspace has a matching fictional example row (Planning registers → "Show fictional example"), and the CSV files in examples/ are labelled fictional. None of them is ever copied into live registers.
## Assumed observations
At the entrance, a mat moves after loaded carts cross it and rainwater tracks around its edge. At one queue fixture, an exposed corner at about 90 cm is contacted by passing carts. Two carts at the corral have broken child-seat straps. The rest of the aisle floor appears intact during this limited walk; that does not establish whole-site safety.
## Proposed response
Isolate the unstable area under the existing procedure and keep a safe accessible route. Remove the two carts from service and log them. Investigate entrance water management and mat fit/securement. Ask facilities to assess relocating or replacing the queue fixture with rounded geometry. Do not begin by covering the store in foam. If a commercial guard is still proposed, review attachment, projection, route width, cleaning and fire requirements before procurement.

## Illustrative rate calculation
**Illustration:** 3 reported injury falls in 150,000 baseline entries and 2 in 120,000 follow-up entries give rates of 2.0 and 1.67 per 100,000. Raw counts and the apparent change do not establish benefit: counts are small, exposure or reporting may differ and no valid comparator is specified. The one-sided 95% upper bound for 2 events in 120,000 entries is about 5.2 per 100,000 — compatible with no change. Record uncertainty and follow the analysis plan rather than advertise a reduction.
