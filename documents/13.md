**Workflow:** W10 (before go-live) and W04 (daily) in document 49.
## Suggested 25-minute shift module
Five minutes: prevention versus cushioning and the actual site hazards, including carts. Five minutes: demonstrate inspection of entrances, seams, guards and routes. Ten minutes: practise a spill response and an isolation/reopening decision, including how to assist a customer when an aisle is closed. Five minutes: report a concern and complete the log. Adapt language, format and duration to workers' needs; include contractors and temporary staff.
## Trainer script
Our purpose is to reduce avoidable hazards for children and everyone else. A soft surface can still slip, move or obstruct wheels. Report defects promptly. Do not improvise repairs or attach household foam to fixtures. Keep the designated safe route open. Signs warn; the site procedure determines how to keep people out of an unsafe area. Only the authorized role reopens the area after checks. Never write a child's name, injury details or contact details into a planning log.
## Competency demonstration
Each learner identifies three hazards, isolates a simulated spill without blocking escape, offers assistance to a customer who cannot use the alternative route, locates the approved cleaning instructions, records all four response times, and names who can authorize restart. Repeat instruction if any critical step is missed. Never practise on a live dangerous spill.
## Record template
Worker internal code (not a name) [ ]; role/shift [ ]; trainer [ ]; date [ ]; module version [ ]; language/accommodation [ ]; demonstration passed or retraining required [ ]; retraining action/date [ ]; supervisor verification [ ]. Maintain worker records in the approved personnel system.
## Refresher triggers
New worker or contractor, changed product or cleaning process, observed unsafe practice, material failure, incident-investigation finding or the site's refresher date. Display a short job aid: Protect people → Assist → Notify → Correct with the approved method → Verify → Log → Reopen by the authorized role.
## Supervisor check
Observe one real inspection and review logs on a risk-based schedule. Address workload and missing supplies; never punish good-faith reporting or use low reported incident counts as the only performance incentive.
