**Workflow:** W10 (go/no-go) in document 49. Needs signed releases.
## What may launch today
The planning program: name owners, approve assessment resources, inspect and correct hazards through existing procedures, maintain carts, organize records and request specialist, authority and supplier input. This launch does not authorize an unreviewed physical installation or an intervention study.
## Physical pilot launch dossier
Index the signed charter; site/zone plan; local applicability and permits; landlord agreement; exact accepted assembly; independent reports and mock-up; approved installation, in-situ verification and commissioning records; training and shift coverage; cleaning/maintenance plan; removal reserve and method; privacy/ethics determination; evaluation protocol; incident contacts; public notice and any notice of temporary disruption; and all open conditions.
## Go/no-go meeting agenda
1. Confirm scope and actual configuration, including substitutions.
2. Review each mandatory criterion and required authority determination.
3. Demonstrate operating readiness and the safe alternative route.
4. Review all adverse findings, dissent and unresolved questions.
5. Confirm data quality, evaluation limits and honest public claims.
6. Confirm budget, maintenance capacity and removal ability.
7. Record a decision: go under stated conditions, revise/retest, defer, or reject/remove — with the decision rights in document 49.
## Non-negotiable no-go conditions — program rules
An unresolved critical safety, access, fire or health condition; a required permission missing; unknown performance of the offered system against a mandatory criterion; no competent operator or maintenance coverage; inability to restore a safe surface; or a research activity lacking the required determination. A sponsor's enthusiasm or a supplier warranty cannot waive these conditions.

## After launch
Publish only the approved factual notice, inspect early operating and cleaning cycles, keep response capacity and review all concerns. Report material adverse findings promptly through the defined channels. Changes reopen affected gates. At pilot end, decide to scale, extend, redesign or remove using document 25 and keep the evidence for future sites.
