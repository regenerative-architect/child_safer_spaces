## Owner and coverage
The site manager owns this procedure; each shift names a responder and a deputy. Set inspection frequency by hazard, weather, traffic and incident history. Proposed planning points: opening, shift handover, closing, and after cleaning, spills or restocking; high-risk zones need more frequent checks. Record the approved interval in the inspection register. **Source fact:** U.S. employers must inspect walking-working surfaces regularly and as necessary and correct or guard hazards before employees use them again [S3].
## Inspection
Walk entrances, transitions, queues, aisles, cart corrals and guards. Check water, debris, curling, movement, seam gaps, loose parts, contamination, new projections and blocked routes. Record time, zone, defect, action and reopening verification. A blank log is not a completed inspection.
## Spill and hazard response
1. Protect people: physically prevent entry to the affected area and keep or create a safe accessible alternative; notify the shift lead. **Source fact:** signs and cones only warn — they do not stop people entering; use barriers, close the area or clean in sections [S2]. Never block an emergency exit.
2. Offer assistance: if an aisle or route is closed, staff retrieve goods or guide customers who cannot use the alternative. U.S. guidance expects merchandise retrieval when an aisle is blocked and temporary interruptions to be remedied as soon as possible [S14][S29].
3. Identify the hazard and call the appropriate trained responder. Unknown chemicals, blood or other specialist hazards use the existing specialist procedure.
4. Remove contamination and fix the source with the approved product, dilution and equipment. Keep the area isolated while wet or unsafe; damp mopped floors can be extremely slippery [S2].
5. Verify dryness, residue, seams, attachment and the surrounding area; record verification before reopening.
6. Log detection, isolation, clean-up and reopening times in the spill register; investigate repeated sources and escalate repairs.

## Escalation
Immediately isolate lifting edges, unstable surfaces, detached guards, blocked access or sanitation failures. Escalate missed inspections, overdue repair and recurrent wetting to the site manager the same shift. Document interim controls and restart under document 14. Keep records under the approved retention policy.
