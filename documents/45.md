## Software acceptance criteria
The release opens without external libraries or network access; role entry points, workflows, filters and full-text search work; documents are editable and recoverable; drafts based on corrected originals are flagged; backups and CSVs import only after validation and preview, with undo; unreadable saved data is quarantined rather than overwritten; v1/v2 data migrate without being modified; gate dependencies are enforced and reopening is explicit; CSV export neutralizes formula-like text; calculations match independent fixtures; invalid inputs never silently produce results. TEST-REPORT.md lists the checks executed and those not verified.
## Content acceptance criteria
No invented partner, insurer concession, code approval, threshold or product efficacy. Every legal and standards statement keeps its scope, edition and retrieval date. Illustrative examples stay separate from live records. Critical unresolved requirements block readiness. Public statements distinguish participation, feasibility and effectiveness. Templates name owners, evidence and the decision they support.
## Unfinished real-world work
Select site and team; verify local requirements; collect baseline and exposure; engage advisors; obtain supplier and independent reports; approve thresholds; secure funding and permissions; install and commission if authorized; train staff; run and evaluate the pilot; make and sign the scale decision. The "Local decisions" list on the Start view and in LOCAL-DECISIONS.md names who decides each item. These are implementation dependencies, not missing documents to be filled with guesses.
