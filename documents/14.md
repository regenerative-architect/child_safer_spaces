**Workflows:** W11 (stop, suspend, withdraw) and W12 (correct and restart) in document 49.
## Incident response
Use the site's emergency procedure and call emergency services when needed. Trained first aiders act within their training; staff do not diagnose injuries. Secure the area and preserve relevant evidence through authorized processes. Notify the manager and the claims contact by the deadlines in the actual policy; record those deadlines in the contact sheet.
## Immediate stop triggers — program rules
Any new unstable or lifting surface; a loose or detached guard or small part; a blocked or materially impaired accessible or escape route; a sanitation failure; an installation-related fire concern; or a serious incident plausibly related to the intervention triggers isolation and suspension of the affected installation pending review. **Program rule:** protective action never waits for proof of causation.
## Who decides — summary of the decision-rights matrix (full matrix in document 49)
| Decision | Who may start it | Who decides | Minimum evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Isolate a hazard | Any trained worker | Shift lead confirms | Observed condition; safe alternative route |
| Suspend an installed system | Any worker, shift lead or reviewer | Site manager | Stop trigger observed; isolation in place |
| Withdraw permanently | Site manager, facilities or evaluation lead | Sponsor | Root cause or unresolved critical risk; removal plan |
| Approve a correction | Facilities lead | Facilities lead with the relevant specialist | Root cause; corrected design; retest plan against pre-agreed criteria |
| Restart after suspension | Facilities lead | Site manager and facilities lead; accessibility reviewer for access issues; authority where required; sponsor for pilot continuation | Retest passed; retraining done; procedure revised |
## Record and investigate
Assign a coded incident ID. Capture zone, time, mechanism, observed conditions, immediate controls, where the witness record is held, and the product batch. Keep identities, photographs showing people, footage and medical or claims information only in protected systems. Preserve originals and chain of custody; avoid speculative blame. Privacy and legal leads set retention and legal holds.
## Withdrawal plan
Facilities arranges competent removal, restores an approved walking surface, checks substrate/adhesive residues and transitions, and manages waste under applicable instructions. Keep the safe alternative route and closure notices in place. Reserve funds and removal lead time before installation. Check other sites with the same batch or assembly; notify the supplier and relevant authorities where required.
## Restart authorization
Document the root-cause assessment, corrected design, retesting against pre-agreed criteria, access/fire/health review as relevant, staff retraining and the revised procedure. Record the decision and rationale in the decisions register even if permanent removal is chosen.
