## Dependencies, not just dates
Dates below are planning targets. Required reviews, procurement evidence and commissioning may take longer. Ordinary hazard correction continues immediately. The 90-day pilot clock starts after G4, not on the day this bundle is opened.
| Workstream | Target | Owner | Required output | Dependency | Workflow |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workspace set-up | Day 1 | Site lead | First backup; acceptance check | None | W01 |
| Sponsor and site scope | Day 1–5 | Sponsor | Charter, budget envelope, stop authority | None | W02 |
| Hazard walk and urgent fixes | Day 1–3, then continuous | Operations | Hazard/action logs, safe routes | Existing safety procedures | W03, W04 |
| Data and stakeholder discovery | Week 1–4+ | Evaluation/community lead | Baseline quality, feedback, exposure plan, adjacent mechanisms routed | Privacy review | W05, W06 |
| Jurisdiction and property routing | Week 1 onward | Facilities/legal | Written requirements and permissions | Exact work scope | W07 |
| Options and test criteria | Week 2 onward | Designer/specialists | Selection rationale and acceptance schedule | Hazard map | W08 |
| RFI, evidence and mock-up | After scope | Procurement | Comparable submissions, failures resolved | G2 pathway | W09 |
| Installation and commissioning | Evidence-dependent | Site manager/facilities | Acceptance, training, handover, signed release | G3 | W10 |
| Feasibility operation | Pilot days 1–90 | Operations/evaluation | Weekly logs and adverse review | G4 | W13 |
| Final review | Day 90 or justified extension | Independent reviewer | Decision memo and public summary | Complete dataset and caveats | W14, W15 |
## During the pilot
Day 1: inspect after initial trading and cleaning; review all concerns. Week 1: verify workflows, material condition and logs daily. Weeks 2–4: weekly joint review; resolve recurring sources; confirm exposure quality. Days 30 and 60: formal operational, budget and access review; revise only through change control. Day 90: assess feasibility; extend effectiveness research if required rather than declare success from small counts.

## Escalation and dependency control
Every task has an owner role, due date, status and evidence. Blocked tasks name the external dependency and the next contact. Changing a task's status or evidence reopens its gate and later gates; changing an owner or date does not. Late work never silently becomes approval. The task board holds non-sensitive planning status; official signatures stay in the organization's controlled system.
