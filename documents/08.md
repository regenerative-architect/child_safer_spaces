## Procurement principle
Specify and test the whole installed system: surface, underlay, seams, trims, adhesive, substrate, fixtures and cleaning method, in the location where it will be used. Any substitution reopens review. **Program rule:** thresholds are written and approved before testing, never chosen after results are seen. No universal friction, impact or rolling-load pass value exists in the sources reviewed [S1][S10].
| Attribute | Required supplier evidence | Site acceptance decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slip behaviour | Named method and edition, validated instrument [S10], wet/dry and representative contaminants, lab and in-situ conditions | Independent specialist sets a method-specific threshold before testing; in-situ check at commissioning |
| Firmness, stability, access | Deformation, secured edges, transition details; full-scale mock-up | Accessibility professional accepts routes and mobility performance |
| Route geometry | Added thickness of guards and padding; resulting protrusion and clear width | Meets the applicable rule (in the U.S., §§307 and 403.5.1 [S28]) at the real fixture |
| Fire | Assembly report matching thickness, adhesive, substrate and use (floor versus fixture) | Code professional or authority confirms the applicable test, class and location [S11] |
| Rolling and static loads | Loaded carts, strollers, wheelchairs, stocking equipment, cleaning machines | No unacceptable rutting, delamination, push effort or instability under approved cycles [S25][S35] |
| Durability | Aged, abraded and wet results; seam and bond data | Criteria, sampling and retest interval fixed in the test plan |
| Hygiene | Compatible chemicals, drying, seams, spill ingress, replaceability | Cleaning lead and, where relevant, the health authority accept a demonstration |
| Edge guards | Attachment, tear resistance, projection, small detached parts, reach | Designer accepts no new snag, ingestion, detachment or collision hazard |
| Impact mitigation | Relevant geometry, energies, body-region surrogate, limits | Specialist accepts the method; no translation into injury reduction without outcome evidence |
| Materials | Traceability, composition disclosures, emissions evidence and SDS where applicable | Relevant product and indoor-air restrictions checked locally |
## Accessibility detail (U.S. example)
**Source fact:** the 2010 ADA Standards require accessible floor surfaces to be stable, firm and slip resistant; carpet must be securely attached with a firm cushion, pad or backing or none, pile no more than 1/2 in, and exposed edges fastened with trim; vertical changes up to 1/4 in are allowed, up to 1/2 in if bevelled no steeper than 1:2, and larger changes must be ramped [S28]. The standards set no minimum friction value [S1]. **Unresolved:** whether a given element is covered, and any stricter state or local rule, must be decided for the actual site.
