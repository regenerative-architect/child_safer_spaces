## Version 3 guide
Version 3.0; issued 20 October 2026. Open index.html after extracting the full bundle, or open standalone.html on its own. The workspace includes 49 documents, workflow cards and decision rights, role entry points, search, editable templates, implementation tasks, sequential gates, 24 editable planning registers, cost scenarios with uncertainty and withdrawal, evaluation planning, the evidence ledger and the source register.
## Records and limits
This is an offline, single-user planning tool. It has no authentication, encryption, tamper-resistant audit trail, organizational approvals or live collaboration. Never store sensitive incident, medical, child, witness, claims or contract information. Browser edits and CSV records are planning copies; official evidence and signatures stay in controlled systems.
## Saving, recovery and transfer
Document drafts save as you type; other fields save when changed. The storage indicator shows whether this browser saved your work. If saved data cannot be read, the workspace keeps an untouched recovery copy and will not overwrite it until you choose. Before a backup import, CSV replacement, row removal or draft restore, an undo copy is kept. Version 1 and version 2 data in the same browser are copied into version 3 and the older copies are left unchanged. Export a JSON backup regularly; different file paths, browsers and devices do not synchronize.
## Drafts and corrected originals
Version 3 corrects several original documents. A draft started from an earlier original is labelled "original updated — review", and the document view can show the differences line by line. Keep the draft, replace it with the current original, or merge by hand.
## Print and source
The original handbook (handbook.html and handbook.pdf) and role packs are static issue copies. Use "Download working handbook" or "Print working handbook" to include local edits. documents/NN.md are the editable sources; source/content.json holds tasks, gates, registers, workflows, decision rights and sources; source/build.cjs regenerates everything; source/tests.cjs and source/browser-tests.py are the automated checks.
## Document control
Assign owner, approver, version, effective date and review date when adopting a procedure. Recheck after incidents, design/material/cleaning changes, new evidence or new rules. Fictional examples are labelled and kept apart from live records. See VERIFICATION.md and TEST-REPORT.md for software checks and remaining limits.
## Completion boundary
The pack supplies the general framework and adaptable work products. It does not supply a selected site, local legal opinion, approved product, signed commitments, insurer concession, grant award, ethics approval or demonstrated injury benefit. LOCAL-DECISIONS.md lists what remains.
