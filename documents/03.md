## Three kinds of work
| Kind | Meaning | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No approval needed | Ordinary safety work and internal planning. In G0 and G1 it can begin today; later tasks of this kind start when their gate is reached | Appoint owners, walk the site, isolate and correct hazards with existing procedures, open registers, request reviews |
| Needs local review | A professional, authority or institution must determine requirements first | Code applicability, accessibility review, privacy and ethics determinations, test criteria |
| Needs signed commitment | An authorized person must sign in a controlled record | Budget, charter, contracts, landlord consent, go/no-go, scale decision |
Each task in the Implementation plan carries one of these labels. A workspace checkbox is never a signature.
## Day 1 — can begin now
1. Sponsor names a site lead and deputy (W02). Record a non-identifying site code, country, state/province and municipality in the site profile.
2. Site manager walks the site with facilities and a worker representative (W03), including accessible routes, wet entrances, queues, cart corrals and fixture edges in the child head-height band (document 28). Correct known hazards through existing safe procedures immediately; never preserve a hazard for research.
3. Open the hazard and action registers. Give every isolated area a safe accessible alternative and a named owner.
4. Assign shift responders and deputies for spills and isolation (W04), and start the handoff log (W16).
5. Export the first planning backup to an approved location (W01).
## Week 1 — begin, then route for review
6. Risk team requests existing de-identified incident trends and visitor counts; the privacy officer decides what may be shared (W05). No child names, injury details or footage enter this workspace.
7. Facilities sends the authority, accessibility and landlord requests in documents 07 and 22 (W07). Silence is never approval.
8. Evaluation lead routes adjacent mechanisms — carts, tip-over, falling merchandise, doors, escalators — to accountable owners (task T35).
9. Finance proposes assessment and removal reserves for sponsor signature (task T03).
## Suggested schedule — planning targets, not permission deadlines
| Gate | Window | Accountable owner | Evidence to advance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G0 Scope | Day 1–5 | Sponsor | Charter, site, roles, immediate hazard controls |
| G1 Baseline | Week 1–4 or longer | Evaluation lead | Hazard map, usable exposure data, adjacent mechanisms routed, privacy/ethics pathway |
| G2 Local pathway | Before any procurement commitment | Facilities/legal lead | Adopted requirements, permits and landlord determinations |
| G3 Product suitability | Before purchase | Procurement lead with specialists | Accepted dossier, approved criteria, mock-up results |
| G4 Installation | Before public use | Site manager | Commissioning, training, maintenance and withdrawal readiness |
| G5 Pilot review | Weekly; formal at day 90 | Steering group | Adverse-event review, operational data, independent analysis |
| G6 Scale | Evidence-dependent | Executive sponsor | Documented benefits and uncertainties, costs, no unresolved critical defects |
## Responsibility matrix
| Work | Accountable | Responsible | Consulted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Budget, stop and scale decisions | Sponsor | Site lead | Finance, insurer, evaluation lead |
| Legal applicability | Business legal lead | Local counsel / code professional | Authorities, landlord |
| Design and installation | Facilities lead | Designer / installer | Accessibility, fire, cleaning specialists |
| Daily controls and isolation | Site manager | Shift and cleaning teams | Worker representatives |
| Evaluation and data | Evaluation lead | Analyst / data steward | Privacy lead, community representatives |
| External claims | Communications lead | Writer | Legal and evaluation leads |
An authority decides within its remit; a consultant or insurer cannot substitute for that determination. Who may stop, suspend, withdraw, restart or scale is set out in the decision-rights matrix in document 49.
