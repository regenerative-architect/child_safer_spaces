Each role's entry point is also on the Start view: choose your role to see what you can do today, what needs local review, what needs a signed commitment, and who you hand off to.
## Business sponsor
Start with 02, 03, 10, 17 and 49. Today: name the site lead and deputies and authorize the assessment. Needs review: legal and insurance conditions. Needs signature: charter, budget envelope and removal reserve. First deliverable: signed charter plus budget envelope. Hand off to: site lead (W02→W03). Do not instruct procurement to buy a material before the suitability review.
## Store manager and workers
Start with 12–14, 28, 36 and 47. Today: walk hazards including carts, assign response coverage, keep safe routes and log defects. Needs review: changes to cleaning chemicals or methods. Needs signature: release of a closed area after a system defect (document 14). First deliverable: current hazard/action list and shift procedure. Hand off at every shift change (W16). Ordinary safety work never waits for a research protocol.
## Facilities, accessibility and fire/code reviewers
Start with 06–08, 29–31 and 11. Today: define the actual assembly and work scope. Needs review: applicable rules and measurable acceptance criteria. Needs signature: commissioning release. First deliverable: coordinated requirements/test schedule with unresolved conditions. Review dimensions and operations at the actual installation, not only drawings.
## Insurer, broker or self-insured risk team
Start with 19, 15–17 and 44. Today: share loss-data definitions and notice requirements. Needs review: coverage and contractor conditions. Needs signature: any premium or term change, in writing. First deliverable: written review questions and policy-specific conditions. No premium reduction is presumed.
## Government and public-health staff
Start with 07, 20, 33 or 34. Today: identify your remit and the correct office. Needs review: determinations within your authority. Needs signature: permits or written determinations. First deliverable: local requirements, an authority response or a policy-review pathway. Taking part in discussion is not endorsement.
## Suppliers, laboratories and installers
Start with 08, 09, 21, 29 and 39. Today: identify the exact configuration and every untested assumption. Needs review: acceptance criteria set by the buyer's specialists. Needs signature: contract and hold-point releases. First deliverable: completed dossier and acceptance schedule.
## Landlord and contractors
Start with 22, 11, 12 and 31. Today: identify alteration permissions, leak/substrate ownership and cart-fleet responsibility. Needs review: compatibility of cleaning methods. Needs signature: responsibility schedule incorporated into actual contracts. First deliverable: agreed responsibility schedule.
## Community advisors and researchers
Start with 24, 30, 32, 35 and 43. Today: share practical access concerns and evidence questions. Needs review: ethics determination for structured testing. Needs signature: data-sharing agreement. First deliverable: feedback themes or an evaluation proposal with limitations. Advisors are never asked to certify safety.
## Everyone
Use the task board for non-sensitive coordination, the gate tracker for evidence references, the workflows view for who decides what, and the document library for role packs. Keep official signatures, personal records and confidential contracts in approved systems. Review G0–G6 together at handovers.
