## Draft review request
Subject: Risk-control review requested for a bounded retail safety pilot

We propose [intervention] at [site] to address [documented mechanisms]. Please review the attached hazard map, baseline incident/exposure summary, exact product dossier, approval pathway, maintenance and training plans, evaluation protocol and removal process.

We request your written comments on relevant coverage conditions, notice obligations, claims documentation, product/contractor responsibilities, and the evidence you would need to evaluate any risk recognition. We are not assuming a premium reduction or that your review constitutes regulatory approval.
## Meeting questions
- Which losses are addressable and which are outside this intervention's mechanism (for example, cart falls versus floor slips)?
- What loss-development period, severity data and exposure denominator would support a credible comparison?
- Does the proposed installation or trial require notification, endorsement or underwriting review under our actual policy?
- What exclusions, deductibles, retentions, reporting deadlines and contractor requirements need counsel or broker attention?
- Can risk-control specialists review wet/dry surfaces, inspection and maintenance?
- What evidence could support future terms or incentives, decided by whom and at what renewal stage?

## Interpretation
One insurer describes analyzing loss history, evaluating walking surfaces and maintenance, and measuring wet and dry slip resistance [S9]. That supports asking for a risk-control review; it does not establish industry-wide approval, an endorsement of any product or a discount. Record insurer feedback as advice or a contract condition as appropriate. A broker conversation is not an insurer commitment unless an authorized party confirms it in writing.
