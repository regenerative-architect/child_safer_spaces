## Four distinct requirement types
LAW: a binding rule applicable to this site. CONTRACT: a lease, insurer or purchase obligation. PROGRAM: an internal criterion adopted for this pilot. PROPOSAL: an incentive or future policy request. Record the type, citation, edition, scope, decision-maker and evidence for every requirement in the jurisdiction register. A "not applicable" entry needs a written reason and a reviewer.

## Canada starting map
Provinces and territories adopt, amend or replace the national model codes; the 2025 national codes are published [S6] but publication does not set the edition at a site. Ontario's 2024 Building Code sets accessibility requirements for most new construction and extensive renovations and does not affect existing buildings unless an extensive renovation is planned [S18]. Ontario's customer-service guidance tells organizations to give public notice when facilities or services that people with disabilities use are temporarily disrupted, with the reason, expected duration and any alternative [S43]; confirm the regulation text (O. Reg. 191/11) locally. CSA/ASC B651:23 is referenced in the National Building Code and other legislation [S27], but whether it binds a site depends on the adopting instrument. Confirm the worker-safety regime, occupiers' liability, public-health requirements and the applicable privacy law with local specialists. Do not apply U.S. ADA dimensions as Canadian legal requirements.
## Fire questions to put to the code official
Model codes treat floor coverings, wall and ceiling finishes, trim, decorative materials, furnishings and foam plastics under different provisions, and a floor-covering report does not answer a fixture question. E648-25a measures one aspect of floor-covering fire behaviour and was developed for corridors and exitways [S11]. Ask: which provisions apply to (a) the floor system, (b) padding fixed to walls or fixtures, (c) freestanding padded items; which test, classification and edition are required for each; and whether the change affects means of egress or sprinkler performance.
