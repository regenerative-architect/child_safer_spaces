## National framework, local determination
Use the ADA and the relevant worker rules as starting layers, then establish state and local building, fire, public-health, liability and privacy requirements for the actual site. The DOJ distinguishes readily achievable barrier removal in existing facilities from alterations, which must be accessible to the maximum extent feasible [S14]. Do not assume an existing store is exempt because no large renovation is planned. Accessible features must be kept in operable working condition, with only isolated or temporary interruptions for maintenance or repair [S29].
## Required routing
1. Operator supplies jurisdiction, ownership, occupancy and intended work.
2. Counsel or an accessibility professional determines ADA and state obligations.
3. Code professional identifies adopted construction and fire editions, local amendments and the alteration classification.
4. Building and fire officials confirm permit and inspection routes within their remit.
5. Health authority reviews food-related zones; the safety lead checks the federal or state-plan worker-safety regime [S3].
6. Property, insurance and procurement parties finalize contractual permissions and acceptance evidence.
## California illustration — not a selected pilot location
California Building Standards Commission Bulletin 25-01 (1 July 2025) states that 1 January 2026 is the statewide effective date of the 2025 California Building Standards Code, that permit applications submitted on or after that date must comply with it, and that the 2022 code continues for applications received by 31 December 2025; local amendments must be ratified and filed for each edition [S17]. A purchase date alone does not set the governing code.
California Title 8, section 3273 ("Working Area") requires permanent floors and platforms to be free of dangerous projections or obstructions, kept in good repair and reasonably free of oil, grease or water, and slippery working surfaces to be protected [S16]. This protects workers; it is not a complete statement of duties to child customers. Ask counsel separately about premises obligations and preservation and reporting rules.

## No universal product approval
Ask whether a floor or guard is subject to product-specific rules as well as building installation requirements. The federal carpet and rug flammability standards [S12] cannot simply be assigned to every resilient material. Classification, warnings, documentation and installation obligations must match the actual product.
## Release criteria
Signed local applicability matrix; permits or a documented "not required" determination; reviewed as-built configuration; required inspections; operator ownership; no critical open conditions. Archive the source and version, and recheck when scope or regulations change.
