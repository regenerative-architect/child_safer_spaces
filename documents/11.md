**Workflow:** W10 in document 49. Needs G0–G3 complete and signed releases.
## Before work
Confirm G0–G3 evidence, permits, landlord consent, accepted drawings and the tested assembly. Record installer competence, work schedule and safe public separation. Confirm storage, ventilation/cure instructions and removal materials. Inspect substrate moisture, flatness, damage and contamination against supplier criteria, and record failures before covering them. Post any required notice of temporary disruption to accessible routes or facilities (in Ontario, see [S43]).
## During installation
Capture batch/lot numbers, surface preparation, adhesive quantities, cure conditions, seam and trim configuration, and every deviation. Keep access and escape routes open or use an approved alternative. Protect adjacent fixtures and food-related operations. Do not accept unreviewed substitutions to solve a schedule problem.
## Before public reopening
- Verify surface bond, seams, movement, transitions, openings and clearances against approved drawings, including route width and protrusions at every guarded fixture.
- Repeat the approved slip-resistance check in situ, on the installed and cleaned surface, with the approved method; record instrument validation and conditions.
- Verify the approved cleaning method, dry condition, cure time and any required tests.
- Demonstrate mobility aids, strollers, loaded carts and cleaning equipment in a controlled setting with consenting adults, including disabled advisors using their own devices where they agree; no deliberate falls and no child impact trials.
- Check guard retention, projections, snagging, tear/detachment and cleaning access.
- Close all critical defects; record non-critical punch-list owners and deadlines.
- Obtain required authority inspections; hand over as-built details, reports, warranty, spare parts, training and the inspection schedule.

## Follow-up
Proposed checks: immediately before reopening, after the first peak trading period, after the first full cleaning, at one week and one month, then risk-based. Increase checks after spills, restocking damage, complaints or unusual loading. These are program defaults that need site approval, not code intervals.
