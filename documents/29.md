## Test matrix
| Test family | Conditions to specify | Results and pass decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traction | Dry; water; representative contaminants; cleaning residue; aged surface; direction; in situ after installation | Method/edition, instrument validation [S10], readings, uncertainty and site-specific acceptance |
| Seams/edges | New and aged; loaded crossing/turning; wetting/cleaning cycles | Gap/uplift/movement, bond failure, photographs and disposition |
| Static/rolling load | Maximum intended load, wheel type/contact area, turning radius, cycles and dwell | Residual indentation, delamination, rolling force and recovery time [S25] |
| Attachment | Exact guard/substrate/adhesive/fastener; foreseeable cleaning and contact abuse | Detachment, tearing, exposed fastener, loose parts and geometry |
| Route geometry | Guard thickness at real fixtures | Protrusion and clear width under the applicable rule [S28] |
| Cleanability | Actual soil, chemical, machine/pad, dilution, drying and seam access | Residue, ingress and damage; specialist-selected sanitation verification |
| Fire performance | Actual floor or fixture assembly and intended location | Required code test/classification and report applicability [S11] |
| Impact attenuation | Relevant surrogate/geometry, energy, substrate and conditioning | Measured force/acceleration and uncertainty; limited to the tested mechanism |
| Access/ergonomics | Routes, transitions, wheels, cane tips, turns, start/stop, loaded equipment | Specialist/user review with predefined tolerances and stop rules |
## Sampling and evidence integrity
The lab proposes replicate numbers from method, variability and decision confidence, and records why samples represent production lots. Record instrument identifiers and calibration, environmental conditions, specimen thickness and the full assembly. Keep failures and raw observations, not only a favourable mean. Test worn and cleaned systems where aging could change performance. Retest after composition or installation changes.

## Limits
F2508 does not convert a friction reading into an injury rate [S10]. Laboratory impact attenuation is a surrogate outcome. Never substitute a playground laboratory claim for whole-system operational acceptance.
