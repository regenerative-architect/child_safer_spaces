# What still requires local professionals, authorities, evidence or signed commitments

Version 4.0. This bundle cannot decide these items. Each blocks the gate shown until the named party decides in writing. Workspace checkboxes are never signatures.

| Decision | Who decides | Gate | Why it cannot be decided here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Adopted building, fire and accessibility rules, editions and local amendments for the actual site | Code professional; authority having jurisdiction | G2 | Editions depend on adoption and timing (S17, S18, S6) |
| Permit and inspection pathway for any physical change | Building and fire officials | G2/G4 | Only the authority can decide within its remit |
| ADA and state access obligations (U.S.) or provincial accessibility and human-rights obligations (Canada) | Accessibility professional; counsel | G2/G3 | Coverage and stricter local rules (S28, S29, S18, S27) |
| Fire classification of floor elements and of padding on walls or fixtures | Code professional; fire official | G2/G3 | Different provisions apply to different elements (S11) |
| Food-zone surface requirements under the adopted food code | Health authority | G2 | Model code adoption varies (S4) |
| Customer premises-liability duties, claims notice and evidence preservation | Local counsel; insurer | G2 | State/provincial law and policy terms |
| Worker-safety regime and any stricter state or provincial rule | Safety lead; counsel | G1/G2 | Federal OSHA, state plans and provinces differ (S3, S16) |
| Landlord consent and allocation of responsibilities | Property owner; counsel | G2 | Must be in the binding lease or contract |
| Acceptance methods and thresholds for the actual installed system | Competent specialists (traction, access, fire, hygiene, materials) | G3 | No universal pass values exist (S10) |
| Independent test reports for the as-installed assembly | Independent or accredited laboratory | G3 | Reports must match the configuration |
| Privacy determination: fields, sharing, retention and any footage use | Privacy officer; counsel | G1 | Applicable privacy law varies (S13, S42) |
| Research-ethics determination for any structured study, participant testing or secondary data research | Institutional REB/IRB | G1/G3 | Pilot studies can be research (S22, S41) |
| Sample size and analysis plan for any effectiveness claim | Independent statistician | G1/G5 | Rare events need large exposure (document 35) |
| Insurance notice, conditions and any premium recognition | Insurer, in writing | G2/G6 | A broker conversation is not a commitment |
| Eligibility for any accessibility tax credit or deduction | Tax adviser | Before budget recognition | Eligibility rules apply (S23) |
| Funding awards | Funder, by written award | Before committed budget | Proposals are not awards |
| Signed charter, contracts, releases, go/no-go and scale decisions | Authorized signatories, in controlled systems | G0/G3/G4/G6 | Workspace checkboxes are not signatures |
| Accessibility and browser compatibility of this workspace in your own environment | Your accessibility and IT staff | Before organizational reliance | Only Chromium was tested here (TEST-REPORT.md) |
| Which translated versions may be used, and who reviewed them | Program owner and a qualified native-speaker reviewer | Before distribution | The French and Spanish briefs (documents 51 and 52) are draft translations that no native speaker has reviewed |
